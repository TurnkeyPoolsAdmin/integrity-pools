# Integrity Pool Builders: design brief

Version 1.0 | November 6, 2026 | Working direction for external designer feedback

Website: https://integrity-pools.vercel.app/

## Assignment
Match the website to the supplied navy and blue Integrity logos. Keep the current layout, section order, spacing, typefaces and responsive behavior. Refine color balance, typography application and logo usage. Return annotated screenshots and proposed visual refinements; keep structural redesign suggestions separate for approval.

## Audience and positioning
Homeowners planning a custom pool, spa or backyard project in Menifee, Murrieta and the Inland Empire. The site should feel clear, confident, warm and professionally managed.

Core offer: professional startup and one free year of pool service with every new pool. Supporting reasons to choose Integrity: a dedicated project manager; coordinated crews; approval before digging; a customer portal with timelines, schedules, permit status, plans, signed permits, equipment manuals and warranties. Portal copy is written for launch at the owner's direction.

## Conversion strategy
The current homepage introduces the offer, explains why early care matters, helps the buyer picture the result, shows who is accountable, explains the free consultation and answers buying concerns. Main action: Get My Free Consultation. Preserve the distinct jobs of the team and consultation sections.

## Logo system
Use the supplied full logo in the desktop/tablet header and footer. Use the square symbol for compact locations: mobile header, browser icon and Apple home-screen icon. Keep the original artwork and gradient. The footer uses a white holding panel. Current web sizes: full logo 280px wide; symbol 52px below 640px viewport; browser icon 32px; Apple icon 180px. Confirm minimum sizes and clear space with the designer. Request vector, white/reversed and simplified small-size masters. Do not recreate the logo lettering using the website fonts.

## Color system
Navy and aqua are sampled from the supplied PNG. Supporting colors are design choices, not pre-existing brand standards. RGB/HEX values are screen specifications; request print color matching separately if needed.

- Logo Navy: #042242 | Primary headings, dark sections
- Ocean Blue: #22658C | Links, buttons, active states
- Logo Aqua: #43A9C5 | Decorative rules and highlights
- Warm Ivory: #F7F5F0 | Hero and alternating surfaces
- Water Mist: #DCEEF6 | Soft highlights and selection
- Sand: #D7BC88 | Small details on dark surfaces
- Deep Blue: #174D70 | Hover states
- Light Aqua: #8CD5E5 | Small text on navy
- Body Slate: #506477 | Paragraph text
- Muted Slate: #607184 | Secondary text
- Sand Ink: #86652E | Review stars on light surfaces
- Control Border: #71879B | Inputs and outlined controls

White and ivory should dominate light sections. Navy provides structure, Ocean Blue signals action, and aqua/sand supply small accents. Use Light Aqua on navy and Sand Ink for review stars on light surfaces. Bright Logo Aqua on white has only 2.72:1 contrast and is decorative, not normal body text. Use Ocean Blue behind white button text. The palette JSON includes all implementation colors and legacy aliases.

## Typography
- DM Serif Display, regular 400: hero and section headings; sentence case.
- DM Sans: body 400, navigation/buttons 500-700, emphatic labels 600-700.
- Hero heading: 48-82px. Most section headings: 36-56px; some CTA headings reach 64px.
- Body and image captions: 16-18px with roughly 1.6-1.8 line height.
- Photo-caption headings: 26-30px bold DM Sans. Buttons and navigation: 14px.
- The original typefaces, sizes and layout have been retained in this color update.

## Accessibility review
Measured pairs: white on navy 16.00:1; white on Ocean Blue 6.35:1; Body Slate on Warm Ivory 5.62:1; Muted Slate on Warm Ivory 4.60:1; Light Aqua on navy 9.72:1. These pairs meet the 4.5:1 normal-text threshold. This is not a complete accessibility audit. Review focus, hover, form selection and error states as well.
Reference: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html

## Photography and proof
Existing reference photography is credited to Aqua Pools & Outdoor Living and must not be presented as completed Integrity projects. Replace it with verified Integrity work before the customer launch. Existing testimonials are pool-care reviews, not new-construction reviews.

## Questions for the designer
1. Do the blues match the logo well, and is the warm accent restrained enough?
2. Does DM Serif Display complement the logo's geometric lettering?
3. Is the small tagline readable at the current logo sizes?
4. Are the captions, forms and calls to action clear on phones?
5. Which logo variants and production files should be added to the final identity kit?

## Requested handoff
Annotated desktop/mobile screenshots, final color specifications, type hierarchy recommendations and production-ready logo variants. Keep the accepted page layout and content strategy intact. Confirm portal readiness and final service details before launch.
